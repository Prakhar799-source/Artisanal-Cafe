Our Philosophy

The alchemy of patience, grain, and terroir.

Atelier Botanica was born from an unhurried reverence for the ritual. We partner directly with smallholder coffee farmers across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala—paying an average of 42% above fair-trade baselines for prized varietals.

Every morning at dawn, our pastry lab rolls out 72-hour sourdough viennoiserie laminated with AOP Charentes-Poitou French butter, filling our sunlit glass conservatory with the intoxicating perfume of caramelized sugar, roasted cardamom, and blooming jasmine.

Daily Schedule

Micro-Roasting & Baking Rituals

We operate on nature's clock. Join us at specific hours of the day to experience fresh morning bakes or our barista cupping flights.

7:30 AM

First-Pour Single Origin Cupping

Our lead roaster dials in the daily micro-lots. First 10 guests receive complimentary tasting cups alongside cupping score cards.

8:00 AM & 12:30 PM

Oven Dispatch: Warm Viennoiserie

Double-batch kouign-amanns, cardamom swirls, and almond croissants pulled piping-hot from our stone deck ovens.

2:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Golden Hour High Tea & Tonics

Botanical espresso tonics, ceremonial Kyoto matcha lattes, and savory gratinée pairings in the sun-drenched courtyard.
